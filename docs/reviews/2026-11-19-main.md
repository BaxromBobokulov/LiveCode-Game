## Focus area A. Overlapping tiles

**Verdict: acceptable for lethality, but a real readability gap. Minor. A small visual fix is recommended, not required for merge.**

Trace:
- Engine (`src/engine/syntaxRush.ts:304-341`, `findTarget` 271): `typeChar` and the collision step never look at tiles. A single is typeable and lethal whenever `rightEdge <= 1280` / AABB overlap, regardless of what is drawn over its tile. So yes, a single whose tile is fully or partly hidden still costs a life (`SyntaxRushCanvas.tsx:394-408`).
- Draw order (`SyntaxRushCanvas.tsx:578-589`): bugs first, then mascot, then unlocked tiles in list order, then the locked tile last. `lockTarget` is the active word, else the nearest visible bug. A word that is nearest (or active) is therefore locked and drawn on top of a single that catches up with it from behind. All tiles share the same bottom line (`y - SYMBOL_TILE_GAP`) and the locked tile is taller (52.8 vs 44), so a covered single tile is covered fully, not just clipped.
- The bug sprite is never hidden: tiles sit 12 px above the bug's top (`drawTile` bottom = `obstacle.y - SYMBOL_TILE_GAP`), and `drawBug` runs before all tiles. The player always sees that a bug is there, just not which key it wants.

Numbers (SCALE 2, cells 18 px, padding 12 px, locked word width = 18n + 32.8, single tile 44, speeds from `syntaxSpeed` and `wordSpeedCap`):
- Fully hidden when the bug centers are within 9n - 5.6 px (`if` 12 px, 5-letter word 39 px, `function` 66 px). Partly hidden within 9n + 38.4 px (`function` 110 px).
- Time hidden = zone width / (single speed - word speed).
  - 5-letter word at run start (relative 150 px/s): fully hidden 0.53 s, partly 1.1 s. At the start the catch-up point is far from the mascot (min gap 2.2 s puts it near x 620), so it is harmless.
  - `function` at the earliest tier 2 (about 8 s, single about 428, word capped at 212): about 0.6 s fully, about 1.0 s partly.
  - Top speed (single 840): `function` about 0.21 s fully, 0.35 s partly; `if` (word 420) under 0.1 s fully.
- Can a hidden single actually cost a life? To hit the mascot (x < 256) while covered, the word must be within the cover half-width of that same x, so the word's own box is within about 0.3 s of hitting too (at most 66 px at 212 px/s for `function`). Both arrive together, and grace (2 s) pops the second for free, so one life is lost either way. The real cost is the 0.4 to 1 s before that: the player is mid-word, the single's letter is unreadable, and its sprite is visibly on a collision course. The player can still finish the word (the single then becomes the nearest, locked and readable) or guess.
- A second, smaller effect: an unlocked word tile that is older in list order is painted under a newer single's tile (the single passes it), so its letters can be partly covered while some other bug is locked. Rare, same cure.

Acceptable? As a lethality problem, mostly yes (the word ahead is about to hit anyway; spec 0003 Consequences line 201 accepts it). As a readability problem it is a genuine, cheap-to-fix wart in a game whose whole point is reading the next key. Making hidden bugs harmless is not better: it would hide a real threat from a player who can see the sprite.

Recommended fix (ONE option): in the draw loop (`SyntaxRushCanvas.tsx:585-589`), when the locked tile is a word, lift any other visible tile that horizontally overlaps it (`|cx - lockedCx| < (tileWidth + lockedWidth) / 2`) up by `lockedHeight + 4 * SCALE`, via an optional `lift` argument on `drawTile`. Cost: about 8 lines, no engine change, one small constant. It keeps AC-10 intact (the locked tile is still on top of every tile it would overlap, because they no longer overlap) and the lifted tile stays above its own bug. Trade: the tile jumps up when it enters the zone (a one frame pop) and stays lifted for under about 1 s. Update spec 0003 Consequences line 201 to say so. If the engineer prefers zero code, leave as is and keep the accepted-tradeoff note.

## Focus area B. index.html

## Minor
### 🟡 Single tile can be hidden under a locked word tile, `src/features/game/SyntaxRushCanvas.tsx:585`
**Problem**: See focus area A. A catching-up single's tile is fully covered by the locked word's larger tile for up to about 0.6 s (up to 1 s partly), while its bug sprite stays visible and its hit stays lethal.
**Why it matters**: A beginner reads the tile to know what to type; an unreadable but dangerous bug feels unfair.
**Suggested fix**: Lift overlapped tiles above the locked word tile (details above), or record it as an accepted tradeoff.

### 🟡 favicon.png grew 4.5x and is unrelated, `src/assets/favicon.png`
**Problem**: 263 KB to 1.19 MB, in the same working tree as the word obstacle change; referenced by index.html on every load.
**Why it matters**: Slows first paint for a tab icon, mixes an asset swap into a feature commit, and may be unintended.
**Suggested fix**: Confirm it is intended; resize or compress; commit separately from spec 0003.
